### S1 contract gate

Specify recovery states before coding: disabled/unsupported memory-only,
opening, verified-ready and failed. Do not turn optional storage into a new
grant prerequisite. While an enabled/supported recovery is pending, absence of a
native latch is **not** evidence of no prior uncertainty. Recovery failure must
not silently become an empty successful document or bypass that distinction;
make the explicit recovery/continuation route and its limits clear.

Uncertainty is monotonic within its evidence generation. Editing, declining
text restoration, autosaving, changing roots and creating another composer do
not resolve it. Keep text/format restoration guarded by exact native ownership
and unchanged empty contents.

**Tests:** extend native persistence/delivery fixtures, real fresh-process restart
tests, provider/batch tests and engine integration tests. Use fake clocks and
controlled callbacks/HTTP cleanup, never real OAuth renewal or People requests.
